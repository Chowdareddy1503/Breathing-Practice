How to breathe: Let your belly gently rise as you breathe in through your nose. Then breathe out slowly and gently through lightly pursed lips. Do not force a deep breath and do not hold your breath.
Important: This is a breathing-practice aid, not an asthma treatment or replacement for prescribed medicines or your asthma action plan. Stop if you feel dizzy, uncomfortable, increasingly breathless, wheezy, or tight-chested, and follow your usual asthma action plan. If symptoms are severe or do not settle, seek urgent medical care.

Why this breathing pattern?
  • It uses slow, comfortable breathing rather than forceful deep breaths.
  • The exhalation is longer than the inhalation and is relaxed.
  • There is no deliberate breath-holding or hyperventilation challenge.
  • Practice when you are breathing comfortably so the technique becomes familiar.
Keep prescribed asthma medicines and your asthma action plan available. This page does not replace medical care.
